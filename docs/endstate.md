# Endstate

Owner-provided statement · September 12, 2026 · preserved verbatim below.

> i’d define endstate as a civilizational attractor: the long-run condition toward which institutions, technology, infrastructure, culture, and governance should converge when we stop treating present scarcity, hierarchy, and coordination failure as fixed constraints. its objective is to maximize durable sentient flourishing across time while minimizing involuntary suffering, domination, ecological destruction, and preventable loss of agency. truth, competence, autonomy, safety, and option value are treated as system properties, not luxuries.
>
> in practical terms, endstate is a civilization where material scarcity has been pushed toward irrelevance through automation, abundant clean energy, advanced manufacturing, and high-capability intelligence; where access to housing, food, healthcare, education, mobility, computation, and meaningful participation is unconditional; where most compulsory labor has disappeared; and where governance works more like continuously improving public infrastructure than periodic contests for control. power is radically legible, distributed, revocable, and constrained. systems are designed around humans and other sentient beings rather than around capital accumulation, inherited institutions, or historical accidents.
>
> the deeper layer is that endstate is not merely a political program or a technologically advanced society. it is a civilization capable of deliberately redesigning itself. it can model consequences across generations, incorporate new moral patients as our understanding of consciousness expands, recursively improve its own institutions, and preserve diversity and individual experimentation without allowing local power to become coercive. cities, ecosystems, economies, interfaces, law, education, family structures, and even everyday objects become parts of one coherent humane substrate.
>
> so i would not treat “endstate” as a literal final state. a truly good endstate is an open-ended operating regime: a civilization that has solved enough of the primitive coordination problems that its dominant activity becomes exploration, creation, understanding, relationship, play, and the cultivation of richer forms of flourishing. the endstate is, in that sense, the point at which civilization becomes capable of choosing what it wants to become.
