## Required fidelity surfaces

- **Fonts and typography:** native system sans, restrained heading scale, regular readable body text, compact secondary labels and tabular comparison numerals. No novelty font, oversized marketing hero in the workspace, or decorative monospace. Long decision text wraps within the document column.
- **Spacing and layout rhythm:** retained the source's parameter rail, main plot/table and interpretation margin. Thin rules, 4px radii and flat surfaces establish groups. The final table has six meaningful rows instead of the source's four mock rows; material use and further detail continue below the first viewport intentionally. The challenge action follows the comparison. No page-level horizontal overflow at 1487, 720 or 390 CSS pixels; wider detail tables scroll within labeled regions.
- **Colors and tokens:** warm/cool neutral canvas `#f6f7f3`, ink `#17251f`, muted `#626e67`, sage action `#21644d`, fine gray-green dividers. Selection uses a restrained surface tint. Series use distinct colors plus labels and a dashed baseline. No glow, gradients, glass or nested card stacks.
- **Image quality and assets:** the reference contains no photographic or illustrative assets to reconstruct. The chart is real model data rendered with Recharts, not a raster imitation. Small conventional controls use the installed Lucide icon family consistently. Source and capture are sharp at native dimensions.
- **Copy and content:** removed unsupported “published research” claims and numeric consent counts from the generated mock. Every displayed result is a modeled repair-need/work/material quantity. Consent, authority, ecological and health limits remain explicit. No winner, approval or overall flourishing score is implied.
